Research interest proposal

Mobility Resilience, Environmental Justice, and Human-Centered Transportation AI

A concise research agenda connecting transportation networks, GIS, environmental exposure, and multi-agent simulation.

Research Direction

My research interest focuses on how transportation systems behave under disruption and how their impacts are distributed across communities. I am especially interested in linking GIS, aviation and transit networks, environmental justice, and LLM-driven multi-agent simulation to evaluate decisions before they become real-world consequences.

Core Questions

  • How do travelers adjust route, mode, timing, and destination choices when networks are disrupted?
  • How can airport and transit operations reduce unequal exposure to emissions, congestion, and safety risks?
  • How can LLM agents be benchmarked against traditional agent-based models for credible transportation planning?

Methods

The proposal combines spatial statistics, network science, geospatial machine learning, street-view perception modeling, emission exposure analysis, and large-scale agent simulation. Python, GIS, OSMnx, NetworkX, PyTorch, and MATSim-style workflows form the technical backbone.

Expected Contribution

This agenda aims to produce decision-support models for resilient multimodal systems, sustainable airport planning, and equitable infrastructure investment. The practical goal is to help agencies and researchers test policy alternatives with transparent assumptions and community-centered metrics.
